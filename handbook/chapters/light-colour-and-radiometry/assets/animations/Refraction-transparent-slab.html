Refraction Through a Transparent Parallel Slab

A fixed 45° ray enters and leaves a parallel-sided transparent slab surrounded by air. The ray bends towards the normal on entry and away from it on exit; because the two faces are parallel, the emerging ray returns to 45° and is parallel to the original ray, but laterally displaced.
