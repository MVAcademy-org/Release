Surface-scale context
Broader manufacturing context: commercial bright/mirror metal finishes can extend into tens or hundreds of nanometres, while rolled, machined, extruded and fibrous surfaces commonly contain micrometre-scale texture. Those surfaces are outside this simplified optical-scatter model.

Scale perspective: surfaces can look perfectly smooth to the eye and still be optically rough. Visible wavelengths are only hundreds of nanometres, so nanometre-scale surface variations can measurably redistribute light long before roughness becomes obvious visually or by touch.
