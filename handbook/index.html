Welcome

The Machine Vision Engineering Handbook is the principal technical knowledge resource of the Machine Vision Academy.

Warning

In order to make content available as soon as it is developed and reviewed, this handbook has been published with structural placeholders. Individual chapters will be released and marked complete as notated in the revision history table below. Chapters with content that has not been marked as released in the Revision History table should not be assumed to be complete nor reviewed.

Who is this handbook for?

The handbook is intended for:

  • engineers designing or specifying industrial vision systems;
  • automation and controls engineers integrating vision equipment;
  • computer-vision and machine-learning practitioners working in industry;
  • students and lecturers studying machine vision;
  • quality, validation and regulatory professionals;
  • technical reviewers evaluating machine-vision projects.

The material is organised so that readers can use it either as a sequential engineering reference or as a source for individual design, implementation and validation topics.
